Personal NAS Sync

Privacy and data use

Personal NAS Sync is a private application used by its owner to synchronize a designated Google Drive folder with the owner's NAS.

Data accessed and its purpose

The sync tool reads file contents and metadata, including names, paths, sizes, modification times and checksums. It uses this information to compare files, transfer changes, check copied content and preserve older versions.

Storage and sharing

Synced files are stored in the owner's Google Drive and NAS. Preserved versions remain in the owner's configured archive locations. Authentication tokens, sync state and operational logs are stored privately on the owner's machine running the sync. The sync service does not publish files or change their sharing permissions.

The owner controls the Google account, the NAS and access to these storage locations. This tool does not send file data to advertising or analytics services.

Retention and owner controls

Active files follow the owner's sync configuration. Older versions and dated backups remain until the owner removes them. The owner can stop the NAS service and revoke the application's Google access through Google Account settings.
